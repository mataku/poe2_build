# Spirit Walker — matalker

Crit spear build with a companion damage layer. Season 0.5 "Runes of Aldur", endgame mapping.

- Character: `matalker` (Huntress / Spirit Walker)
- Profile: https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/matakuchan-0487/runesofaldur/character/matalker

## Core concept

Spear attacks scaled through crit chance and crit damage bonus, with a permanent companion doing a second stream of damage. **Inspiring Ally** is the hinge: increases to companion damage also apply to the character, so companion-damage passives are dual-purpose. Elemental conversion (Sacred Flame, Trinity, Herald of Ice) turns physical spear damage into an ailment-heavy elemental profile that The Taming then multiplies.

Defence is evasion + energy shield hybrid with no keystone — nothing forces a build-around, so gear flexibility is high, but there is no single big mitigation layer either.

## Axis

| Axis | Choice |

## Key skill setups

- **Spear Throw** — Rapid Attacks + Lightning Attunement
- **Twister** — Elemental Armament + Prolonged Duration + Projectile Acceleration + Retreat + Life Leech
- **Whirling Slash** — Rapid Attacks + Rage + Heightened Accuracy + Knockback
- **Companion: Mighty Silverfist** — Elemental Army + Rapid Attacks + Muster + Brutality
- **Wild Protector** — Last Gasp + Hulking Minions + Elemental Armament + Muster
- **Barrage** — Prolonged Duration + Cooldown Recovery + Heightened Charges + Perpetual Charge
- Utility: Herald of Ice, Trinity, Righteous Descent, Primal Bounty, Whirlwind Lance, Freezing Mark, Purity of Fire (Precision + Clarity)

## Passive tree

No keystone allocated.

Main clusters:

- **Crit damage** — Javelin, Heartbreaking, For the Jugular, Deadly Force, Heartstopping, True Strike, Struck Through (a lot of flat crit-damage-bonus small nodes on top)
- **Spear** — Focused Thrust, Precise Point, Roll and Strike, Maiming Strike
- **Projectile / speed** — Precision Salvo, Honed Instincts, Clean Shot
- **Companion** — Inspiring Ally plus the companion-damage small nodes (these scale the character too)
- **Defence** — Patient Barrier, Inner Faith, Blur
- **Sustain** — Combat Alchemy and the flask-recovery smalls

## Gear axis

- **The Ordained** (Grand Spear) — 216% increased physical damage, +7.47% to crit chance. The crit chance on the weapon is what makes the whole crit tree pay off.
- Offhand **Sacred Flame** (Shrine Sceptre) — 57% of damage as extra fire, Presence buffs for the companion (allies gain 22% of damage as extra fire, 2.5% life regen).
- Weapon swap: **Skysliver** (Winged Spear) — 24% attack speed, 50% increased shock chance.
- **The Taming** — 21% increased damage per elemental ailment type on the enemy; pairs with the shock/freeze/ignite spread from Trinity + Herald of Ice.
- **Essentia Sanguis** gloves, **Foe Heart** amulet (+47 Spirit, feeds the companion/herald reservation).
- Physical attack leech from the Glyph Knuckle ring is the main recovery.
